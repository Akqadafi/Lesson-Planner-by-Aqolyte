ABOUT AQOLYTE

Technology should make work clearer.

Aqolyte LLC develops focused digital products for learning, work, and decision-making. We begin with a real task, design around the person doing it, and use AI only where it adds practical value.

OUR DIRECTION

Useful by design.

Aqolyte sits at the intersection of web development, educational technology, and applied AI. The company’s work is guided by a simple standard: a product should help someone complete an important task with more clarity and control.

Our first product, Aqolyte Lesson Planner, applies that standard to teacher planning. It organizes lesson generation into an editable workflow, supports student materials, and keeps review and approval with the teacher.

WHAT GUIDES US

Three working principles.

01

Serve the task

Start with the user’s goal and remove friction from the path to completing it.

02

Show the limits

Describe what a tool can verify, what still needs review, and where uncertainty remains.

03

Keep control human

Make work editable, recoverable, and reviewable before it is shared or exported.
